# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences with equal weight:
- **Recruiters and hiring teams** evaluating Alain for a .NET / full-stack developer role. They scan quickly: current role, years of experience, stack, past work, CV.
- **Freelance clients** looking for someone to build a web or mobile app. They want to know what he can build, see proof, and how to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Alain Pereida Prado (pereidanet.me). It presents who he is, his experience, projects and stack, and turns a visit into a contact, a CV download, or a freelance inquiry. Bilingual: Spanish (default, `/`) and English (`/en`).

## Positioning

A working developer with 5+ years shipping production software across .NET backends, Vue/Express web apps and Flutter mobile apps — university systems at UABC, a drone-monitoring platform, APIs serving 100,000+ users, and current .NET work at Reliant Parking.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Astro 4 + Tailwind 3, static site, i18n via `src/i18n/es.json` and `en.json`.
- Sections: hero, about + contact, freelance services, projects, experience, grouped stack.
- Downloadable CVs in `public/documents/` (ES and EN).
- Light/dark theme toggle and sticky navigation.
